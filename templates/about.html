About AstroGuide

AstroGuide is a simple, friendly place to explore zodiac astrology: browse the twelve signs, find your sun sign from your birth date, read a sample daily horoscope and compare two signs.

Zodiac astrology in brief

Western astrology divides the year into twelve signs of roughly a month each, based on where the Sun appears against the sky on your birthday. Each sign has an element (Fire, Earth, Air or Water) and a ruling planet, which traditionally shape its described personality.

Astrology is a cultural tradition, not a science. Everything here is general entertainment content, not a verified prediction.
